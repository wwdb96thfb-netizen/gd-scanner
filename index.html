Help

How to scan a vehicle
  1. Choose your post under Location. It stays chosen for the next scan.
  2. Tap Take photos and photograph, in any order: the GD and every other paper, the number plate, the goods, and the driver's CNIC front and back. Take them all together; the app works out which is which.
  3. Keep each photo sharp and filling the frame. A blurred or dark photo is marked red and cannot be sent: remove it with the × and take it again.
  4. Choose the goods found on the vehicle and enter how much.
  5. Tap Send for check. You can start the next vehicle straight away.
  6. The result comes to your screen as a card with the vehicle photo and number. Follow the order on it.
  7. The app reads the driver's details from his CNIC, front and back. Nothing is typed. If it was missed, add it later inside the file under Driver.

No signal? Send anyway. The scan waits on the phone and goes up by itself.

Your earlier scans are listed under Past cases, below the Send button.

The three orders

Release چھوڑ دیں: the papers are in order, or an officer has ordered release. Let the vehicle go.

Detain روک لیں: stop the vehicle and do not let it go. The file lists what may be wrong in the papers. Wait for the officer's order.

Seize ضبط کریں: only on an officer's order. Unload the goods to the warehouse.

Only an officer decides. When he does, the notice on your phone changes to his order. Tap Confirm: decision carried out when you have done it.

Every order shows the vehicle photo and number. Check both before you act.

For officers: deciding a case

A new case shows Detained, blinking, with Release, Detain, Seize buttons on its tile. You can decide there, or open the file first.

The file opens with a Suggestion and one line of reason. Tap Why to read the points found. Confirm a critical point on the paper and the goods before you seize: a blurred photo can cause a false one.

Your name is recorded with every decision. On whose order is optional. For Detain, write what the post must do or which papers to get.

What can be changed later: a Detain can become Release or Seize. A Seize can only become Release. A Release cannot be changed, except by the Owner to correct a mistake.

A clean scan is released by the system itself and needs no decision.

Finding your way

Database: Current cases are those still waiting for a decision. Past cases are those already decided or released by the system.

Bell: the red number is how many notices you have not read. Tap a notice to go straight to its case or to the person it is about.

Hold a finger on a case to share it or delete it. What is shared carries the Coast Guards band and leaves out decisions, the points found and the driver's CNIC.

Manage: the day's summary, members, the watchlist and phone alerts. A watchlist entry shows the vehicle or the identity card and opens onto its papers.

Checking a paper yourself

GD: a real one is printed from the Customs system. It has numbered boxes, a machine number in box 58, a payment number in box 65, and a line at the foot saying it is a system generated document. It needs no stamp or signature.

Sales tax invoice: from 2026 it should carry an FBR invoice number and a QR code. Scan the QR code with the FBR Tax Asaan app, or send an SMS to 9966, to see whether FBR knows it.

Plant Protection papers: system prints carry a QR code. Scan it with the phone camera.

Auction papers: goods leave an auction only on a delivery order with a seal, a serial number and a lot number. Ring the office that held the auction and confirm the lot and quantity.

Letters, NOCs and permits: these cannot be checked from the paper. Ring the issuing office on a number you find yourself, not one printed on the letter.

Checking the load

A copy of the GD is normal. One GD can cover several vehicles. What matters is that all the loads together stay within the GD, so always enter the goods found.

A part load should carry: a copy of the GD, the importer's sales tax invoice or delivery challan for that quantity, and a bilty for this vehicle.

Count and weigh. Packages × weight on each should agree with the invoice and the bilty.

Read the packing. Product name, "Made in" and the manufacturing date. Goods made after the GD date cannot belong to it.

Open packages from the middle and the back, not only near the door.

Ask the driver: who owns it, where it was loaded, where it is going, how many trips on this GD. The answers should match the bilty.

Rules for your account

One phone. Your account works on one phone only. For a new phone, an officer taps "Allow new phone" beside your name.

PIN. Asked each time the app opens. If you forget it, an officer resets it and you choose a new one.

Your name. Ask for a change under Your account. It takes effect when an officer approves it.

What you can see. Post Staff see only their own scans. Officers and Bn Staff see all. Bn Staff cannot decide or change other people's files: they are shown "Restricted Access".

Removed. If an officer removes you, the app closes on your phone and forgets your PIN. It opens again only when you are added back.

Location. The phone's location is recorded with every scan.

Photos are official property. Each photo is marked "Pakistan Coast Guards" with your name, post and time. When a photo is opened, the viewer's name is shown over it.

About this app

GD Verification System helps Pakistan Coast Guards posts check goods on the road against their customs papers, quickly and in the same way at every post.

The server reads the photos, checks the papers against each other, against the goods found and against every earlier file, and tells the post what to do.

It records who scanned, where and when, what was found, every decision and who gave it.

The app gives guidance from the papers. It can be wrong. The officer on the spot and the chain of command remain responsible for the decision.
