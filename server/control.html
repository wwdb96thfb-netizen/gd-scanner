Move the server to another Mac

  1. Make the transfer file. One file with the whole server: cases, photos, members, links.
  2. Take it to the new Mac on a USB drive, double-click it, and open "0 - NEW MAC - START HERE". The steps are also in the "READ ME FIRST" file beside it.
  3. When the new Mac is working, retire this one. It stops for good, so two servers never run together.
    Retire this Mac as the server?

    Do this only after the new Mac is running and a test scan has worked. This Mac stops serving at once and will not start again by itself. The data stays in the GD-Scanner folder until you delete it.

Members keep their phones, links, usernames and PINs. Do steps 1 to 3 on the same day: scans made here after the file is made are not in it.

Hand over ownership

Makes another Officer the Owner. You become an Officer. He then signs in to this Operations Room with his own username and PIN.

  • He gets every Owner power: this Operations Room, removing officers, correcting releases.
  • You stay in the system as an Officer, with your phone, username and PIN unchanged.
  • This window locks at once. Only he can open it afterwards.
  • It cannot be undone from your side. Only he can hand it back.
