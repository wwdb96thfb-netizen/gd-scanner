GD Scanner

Customs document check for the post

  1. PhotographTake photos of the papers, the vehicle and the goods, in any order.
  2. SendThe server reads every paper and checks it against the others and against earlier files.
  3. ActYou are told what to do: clear to go, ask for documents, hold or detain.
  4. RecordNote what you did with the vehicle. Everything is kept for your officers.

Works without internet: scans wait on the phone and go up by themselves. Your location is recorded with every scan, and the app is protected by your own PIN.

Location sharing is required

GD Scanner records where each scan is sent from, so that every check can be tied to its post. Your location is read only at the moment you press Send. The app does not work without it.

Daily summary

Watchlist

Enter a vehicle number, importer name, NTN, GD number or container number. Any file that matches is marked Critical and the post is told to detain.

Minimum customs values

Enter figures from the official valuation rulings. A GD that declares less is flagged. Use an HS code (at least 4 digits) or a word from the goods description.

What the results mean

Detain: a critical point failed. Do not release; inform your officer.

Hold: figures do not agree. Retake sharp photos and send again; if still red, detain.

Ask for documents / Question driver: release only after the papers or answers listed are given.

Clear to go: papers are in order. Match goods and vehicle number by eye, then release.

After acting, record what you did under "What did you do with this vehicle?" in the file.
